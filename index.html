About Steno Master

A Decade of Shorthand Excellence

Serving Hisar City Since 2014

  • Most Renowned Steno Institute of Hisar
  • Hindi & English Shorthand Experts
  • 100% Dedicated to Government Job Preparation
  • Hundreds of Students Trained for High Court
  • Pitman Certified Teaching Methods

Operating from the heart of the city at Lahoria Chowk, Hisar, Haryana, Steno Master has established itself as the leading specialized stenography academy. Over the past 10 years, our core focus has been helping aspirants master shorthand to clear speed skill assessments for government departments.

We break down complex rules into simple, reproducible outline patterns, ensuring students write with high velocity while maintaining absolute readability during transcription checks.

Mission

To deliver systematic, high-accuracy shorthand instruction helping students qualify for public jobs.

Vision

To build the most trusted, tech-forward skill center in Haryana.

Expert Faculty

Mentorship by speed writing champions with deep exposure to exam transcription criteria.

Proven Results

A decade-long registry of student placements in High Courts and central secretariats.
